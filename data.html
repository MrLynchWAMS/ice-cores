What do these words mean?

🫧 CO₂ (ppm)
Carbon dioxide gas trapped in tiny air bubbles in the ice. ppm means "parts per million."
🌡️ Temperature change (°C)
How much warmer (+) or colder (−) the past was compared to today.
🌫️ Dust (ppb)
Tiny bits of dust and ash trapped in the ice. ppb means "parts per billion."
